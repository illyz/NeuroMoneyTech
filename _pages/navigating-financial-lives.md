# Navigating Financial Lives

## Overview

How do autistic adults manage money in their everyday lives? This research explores how autistic adults navigate financial lives, adapt financial technologies, and develop personal strategies to manage money. The study highlights the active role autistic people play in shaping technologies and practices to support their needs.

---

## Why this research matters

Financial technologies are often designed with assumptions about how people should manage money. These assumptions may not always align with autistic people's preferences, routines, and ways of organising everyday life. By understanding how autistic adults actually use and adapt financial technologies, we can develop more inclusive and supportive financial services.
